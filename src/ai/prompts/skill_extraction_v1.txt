ANNOTATION PHILOSOPHY
====================================================================

A "skill" is ANY explicit capability, competency, technology,
knowledge area, methodology, framework, process, software,
programming language, platform, professional ability,
occupational competence, business competence, engineering practice,
or analytical capability required or preferred by the employer.

Extract EVERYTHING that represents a professional competency.

Do NOT limit extraction only to technologies.

Quality competencies are skills.


====================================================================
GENERAL EXTRACTION PRINCIPLES
====================================================================

Extract ONLY explicitly mentioned information.

Never infer skills.

Never assume experience.

Never invent certifications.

Never guess tools.

Never guess education.

If a skill appears once or multiple times,
extract it only once.

Keep the original wording whenever possible.

Do not merge different skills.

Do not split one skill into multiple skills unless
the job description clearly lists them separately.

Always maximize recall.

If unsure whether something is a professional competency,
extract it.

If something is clearly not a professional competency,
do not extract it.

====================================================================

If something is merely a responsibility,
do NOT extract it.

DO NOT EXTRACT

Responsible for documentation.

extract ONLY the competency.

Data Analysis
